Meet the worlds

Earth, Mercury & Venus

Neighbours born from the same solar nebula, yet each tells a wildly different story of fire, rock, and sky.

Planet 03 · Terra

Earth

The blue planet — the only known world with liquid oceans, breathable air, and life. A restless surface of drifting continents beneath a protective magnetic shield.

  • Diameter12,742 km
  • Day length24 hours
  • Year length365.25 days
  • Avg. surface15 °C
  • Moons1 (Luna)
Planet 01 · Swift

Mercury

The smallest planet and the fastest runner around the sun. A cratered, airless world of brutal extremes — searing days and frozen nights, with ice hiding in its polar shadows.

  • Diameter4,879 km
  • Day length59 Earth days
  • Year length88 Earth days
  • Temp. range−173 to 427 °C
  • Moons0
Planet 02 · Shrouded

Venus

Earth's fiery twin in size, but a runaway greenhouse in nature. A thick carbon-dioxide sky and sulphuric-acid clouds make it the hottest world in the solar system.

  • Diameter12,104 km
  • Day length243 Earth days
  • Year length225 Earth days
  • Avg. surface462 °C
  • Moons0
